# Point-in-time recovery

RailDock exposes PITR configuration and removal endpoints as part of the service recovery surface. PITR support and exact semantics depend on the datastore and recovery backend.

## Before enabling PITR

- Confirm the datastore type/version is supported by your RailDock release.
- Configure and verify an appropriate recovery destination.
- Confirm retention and storage expectations.
- Run an isolated recovery drill before relying on PITR for production.

## Recovery preferences

Service recovery preferences are separate from the base service record. Keep recovery policy explicit, review it after datastore upgrades, and include the recovery path in your operating runbook.
