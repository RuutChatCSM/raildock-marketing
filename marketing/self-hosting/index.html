Operational boundary

What RailDock owns—and what you still own.

Self-hosting gives you more control, but it also keeps host security, capacity planning, networking and upgrade discipline in your domain.

RailDock owns orchestration

Project lifecycle, service operations, desired-state planning and application-level workflows.

You own the host boundary

Operating-system hardening, firewall policy, capacity and network reachability remain your responsibility.

Secrets stay in your environment

Application secrets, keys and infrastructure credentials remain inside the infrastructure you operate.
